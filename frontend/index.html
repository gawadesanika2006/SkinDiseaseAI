Melanoma ABCDE Visual Guide

The clinical standard for evaluating atypical pigmented lesions for early melanoma detection.

A

Asymmetry

One half of the mole or lesion does not match the shape, elevation, or outline of the opposite half.

B

Border

The edges are irregular, ragged, notched, scalloped, blurred, or poorly defined against normal skin.

C

Color

Color is not uniform throughout; shades of tan, brown, black, or patches of pink, red, white, or blue.

D

Diameter

The spot is larger than 6 millimeters across (roughly the size of a standard pencil eraser), though melanomas can be smaller.

E

Evolving

The mole is noticeably changing in size, shape, surface texture, elevation, or symptomatically bleeding or itching.

About the SkinNova AI System

Technical architecture, machine learning pipeline, and algorithmic safeguards.

🧠 Deep Neural Network Architecture

The system employs a ResNet-18 (Residual Network) convolutional neural architecture fine-tuned specifically on dermatoscopic skin lesion datasets. Input images are converted to 224×224 normalized RGB tensors and passed through 18 convolutional layers with identity skip-connections.

Layer Quantization: INT8 Dynamic Latency: ~250ms CPU Classes: 7 Distinct Pathologies
